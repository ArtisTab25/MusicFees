MusicFees modifies the file at build time (scripts/soundfonts.mjs) so it plays correctly in
alphaTab: stereo sample pairs become panned mono samples, initial attenuation is scaled to
40 % (FluidSynth behaviour), velocity-to-filter modulators are baked in at velocity 100, and
global-zone attenuation is compensated.
